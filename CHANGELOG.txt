1.12.0
- Smaller downloads. There are now two Windows editions: Core (~110 MB, down from 241 MB) with everything most people need - scan, analyze, organize, play, tag - and Full (~185 MB) which adds YouTube download and the Essentia key vote.
- Both editions still detect BPM and key, keep your files safe, and need no separate Python. Core uses the neural-net and NumPy key voters (16 of 17 of your beats matched Tunebat in Full, 15 of 17 in Core).
- Dropped a library (scikit-learn) that was being packaged but never used.
- Updates to a downloaded copy no longer try to reinstall packages, so they're faster and work offline.

1.11.0
- Polished the player controls: the play/pause, stop and skip buttons are now crisp icons that stay put (no more jumping when play turns to pause).
- You can make and color-code tags without selecting a beat first: click + Tag, name it, pick a color. Your tags show in their colors in the filter sidebar. Right-click a tag in the + Tag window to delete it.
- Resizable: drag the divider between the filter sidebar and the beat list on the Library tab (your position is remembered), and the Settings, Fix, Find-duplicates and FL-sync windows can now be resized.

1.10.0
- No Python needed: download BeatSorter-Windows.zip from GitHub Releases, unzip it, and double-click Beat Sorter.exe.
- Closing Beat Sorter now stops everything right away. Before, closing during a scan left the analysis running in the background until it finished.
- Fixed: the very first scan on a computer could fail for every beat (the audio library's one-time setup crashed when several workers did it at once). It now runs once on its own, and a crash is retried instead of failing the scan.
- Much less CPU while the app is open and idle (the header animation rests when nothing is happening).

1.9.4
- Tidier names in the Sorted folder: "Nasty_142bpm_Cm" shows as "Nasty [142 BPM · C#m]". Underscores become spaces, and the BPM/key already in a name are shown once, in the same style for every beat. Your original files keep their names.
- FL Studio's tags follow automatically when links in Sorted are renamed or moved.

1.9.3
- Report a problem (top right): send a problem or idea as a GitHub issue or an email, with technical details you can see first. Error messages offer it too.
- Safer updates: if a new version doesn't open, the previous one is put back automatically. Leftover helpers are closed before files are replaced.
- Updates now come only from versions that passed automatic tests on a real Windows PC.
- Fixed: after a failed update, the message could wrongly say the old version was restored.
- Fixed: cancelling a scan could briefly remove the unscanned beats from your Sorted folder.
- Fixed: downloaded beats now land in renamed folders right away, and re-downloads don't re-analyze.
- Ctrl+arrow keys no longer move the player while you're typing in a search box.

1.9.2
- Fixed: an update could leave Beat Sorter unable to open on Windows (a helper program still had the app folder open while it was being replaced). Updates now wait until the folder is free and never leave it half-replaced.
- If Beat Sorter ever can't start, it now says why instead of closing silently.

1.9.1
- New player bar at the bottom of every tab: play/pause, stop, previous/next beat, a seek bar you can click or drag, volume, Loop and Autoplay.
- Fixed: a beat started from the Sorted tab couldn't be stopped there.
- Keys: Space play/pause, Esc stop, Ctrl+Left/Right jump 10 seconds, Ctrl+Up/Down previous/next beat.

1.9.0
- New Sorted tab: your library as a folder tree. Drag beats onto a BPM/key folder to move them (saved as your fix), into your own folders, or onto a tag.
- My Folders: make your own folders (nested, any names, any order), e.g. Album ideas or Send to artists. They show up in Sorted\My Folders for FL Studio. Optional numbering keeps your order in FL.
- Change the folder layout: BPM > Key, Key > BPM, Camelot > BPM, BPM only, Key only, or one folder.
- Rename any folder, including the automatic ones (e.g. 140-149 BPM to Fast trap).
- Changes update the Sorted folder right away; only what changed is touched.
- Your beats load as soon as the app opens; only new or changed beats get analyzed.

1.8.0
- Essentia, the open-source engine behind many online key finders, now votes on every key. It runs through deno (already installed for YouTube downloads); if it can't run, the other detectors carry on without it.
- On your beats, checked against Tunebat: 16 of 17 keys right (1.7.0: 15, 1.6.0: 10).
- Beat Sorter is now free and open source under the GNU AGPL v3 (see LICENSE).
- Your library is re-analyzed once after updating.

1.7.1
- Fixed "Create desktop shortcut.bat" (it failed with PowerShell errors).

1.7.0
- Much better key detection: three detectors now vote (a neural network trained on thousands of tracks, plus two Essentia-style key finders, the kind of engine online key sites use). On your beats, checked against Tunebat: 15 of 17 right, up from 10 of 17.
- About 3x faster analysis (around 3 seconds per beat).
- "Name may be off": when all three detectors agree on a different key than the file name, the Notes column says so. Right-click > Use detected key fixes it in one click.
- BPM range is learned from your named beats after each scan (on your beats: 89% exact, up from 61%). Turn off in Settings.
- Key names like D#m, G#m and A# are now understood everywhere.
- Your library is re-analyzed once after updating, because detection changed.

1.6.0
- Sync tags to FL Studio: your tags, BPM ranges, keys and one-shot types show up as filters in FL's own Browser (More > Sync tags to FL Studio). Keeps the tags you made in FL.
- Fix BPM / key: correct a wrong key or BPM, or halve/double a BPM. Your fixes win over names and detection.
- Compatible keys: show beats in keys that mix with the one you pick (Camelot wheel).
- Find duplicates: same file twice, or the same beat as WAV and MP3 (More > Find duplicates).
- One-shots sorted by type: Kicks, 808s, Snares, Hi-Hats, Claps, Cymbals and more.
